## Design Context

### Users

A solo operator uses Gas City's dashboard to move Beads through a work queue, inspect execution attempts, and coordinate active sessions and PRs. Workbench is the operational companion to Beads Canvas, not another task store.

### Brand Personality

Compact, calm, and dependable. The interface should make live operational state legible without implying an action succeeded before Gas City confirms it.

### Aesthetic Direction

Carry forward the existing Beads Canvas dark/teal language: restrained surfaces, dense but readable controls, and clear state contrast. Avoid decorative glow or a separate visual identity for Workbench.

### Design Principles

- Keep the selected Bead, attempt details, and actions visible together; avoid overlays that hide the work context.
- Present backend policy, conflict, queue, and acknowledgment states explicitly before enabling consequential actions.
- Give pointer, keyboard, and touch users equivalent ways to change board state.
- Make queue order and filters inspectable and earlier attempts directly selectable.
